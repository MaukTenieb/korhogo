Notable meetings — Childhood, concert-pianist grandmother, door closed on yours truly, entire pieces. Oh, and a lot of comics along the way (Josep María Beà's Taberna Galáctica, John Wagner's Strontium Dog...) Plus André LeBlanc's gorgeous Bible (I love theology). Hawkmoon old-school RPG too (thanks for the virus!). And Philip K Dick's elaborate oddity — of course. Late 1990s? Random workmate put me onto WordSound tracks while, around that time, Ångstrom Records co-funder — in our boring hometown — played some IDM and noise music in his tiny flat. (Dude was this towering bearded guy.) Out of the fanzine melting pot came a profound friendship with a future Marvel penciller I met in a downtown comics shop. Both shop and relationship sadly vanished around the same time I guess. Now, some shitty, soulless clothes place — mh. Early 2000s? Joel Peter Witkin (live interpreting gig I took on the spot): wine with this Christianity aficionado, intimate/intricate/incarnated thoughts; tons of drawings on the restaurant tablecloth. Then started a novel, about a wig-wearing writing dog, with a serious Spanish fella who almost won the 1994 Nadal Prize and "sold four times more than the actual winner". Later, in Budapest-Ithaka, anonymous (and silent) Cigány (Roma) Ulysses walking down the street during — I remember the vertigo — what I must call a Meursault episode. Late 2010s, prominent Magnum member I spent time with called my visual work "intense and strong". More recently? Fire Beninois, an outrageously talented French protean artist, and our shared Tsimtsum electronic crazy-lore. Yep.
